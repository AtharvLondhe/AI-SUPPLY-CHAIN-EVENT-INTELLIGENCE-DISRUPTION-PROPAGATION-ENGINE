# MVP Scope
# Domain : Consum Electronics (mobile/laptop) manufacturing supply chain.
## In scope (MVP)
- Supplier, component, product, factory and logistics entities
- Historical and current structured supply-chain data (sample / synthetic)
- External event dataset (controlled feed)
- Event extraction with NLP
- Entity resolution pipeline
- Graph-based dependency representation (Neo4j)
- Rule-based baseline propagation plus one ML impact model
- Dashboard with path-level explanations
- At least 3 what-if scenarios
## Out of scope
- Autonomous procurement decisions
- Automatic supplier switching
- Direct control of logistics systems
- Guaranteed prediction of geopolitical or natural events
- Replacing enterprise ERP or procurement platforms

Kickoff: 19 Nov 2026. Final submission: 15 Jan 2027.
